# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. This is a small project maintained by one person
in their spare time.

## Specifics

**On health data.** Issues here may contain someone's sleep, heart rate or
training history. Treat anything a person shares as personal, do not repost it
elsewhere, and do not press for more detail than a report needs.

**On being wrong.** This project is full of estimates from a consumer sensor,
and several of them are documented as unreliable. Saying "I think this number
is wrong, here is what I measured against it" is welcome and is how the deep
sleep investigation started. Saying "this is broken garbage" is not useful to
anyone.

**On the upstream project.** Most of this application is
[OpenStrap](https://github.com/OpenStrap)'s work, given away for free after a
great deal of careful effort. Criticism of technical decisions is fine.
Disparaging the people who did that work is not.

**On scope.** "This does not do X" is not a bug. This is a personal fork with
no roadmap and no obligation to anyone. A polite feature request is fine; an
entitled one will be closed.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
Publishing someone's private information. Deliberately wasting maintainer time.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, lock, or delete anything, and block anyone, without
discussion. There is no appeals process, because there is one maintainer and
this is a hobby project.

To report something, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer
directly rather than escalating in public.
